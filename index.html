About Me

M

Sports have always been part of my life, from playing basketball and baseball throughout school, to still being involved with my high school team today. After more than 5 years training and coaching on the floor of a strength gym, working with people from every background, I found the work that mattered most: helping kids and young adults with developmental disabilities build strength, coordination, and confidence through exercise and sports.

That focus didn't come out of nowhere. Growing up, I was active with groups such as Etta Israel and Friendship Circle. Those experiences shaped how I think about ability, connection, and community long before I became a coach.

Today, I work one-on-one with kids and young adults with developmental disabilities, building each plan around the individual: their interests, their pace, and their goals. Whether it's a first strength-training session, learning to shoot a free throw, or simply having a consistent, fun hour of movement each week, sessions meet each person exactly where they are.
